# LA0059: Two Advices of the Same Kind in One Group

| | |
|---|---|
| **Severity** | Error |
| **Reported by** | PostSharp |
| **Message** | `Conflicting advices in aspect "<Aspect>": the methods "<FirstAdvice>" and "<SecondAdvice>" are advices of the same kind in the same group, but a group can have only one advice of each kind. Merge the two advices, or give one of them a group of its own, with another value of the Group property or with a pointcut and no Group property.` |

## Cause

PostSharp weaves the advices of a group as one transformation: the method boundary advices of a group become one `try` / `catch` / `finally` construct, and the interception advices of a group one interception of the method, property or event. A group can therefore contain only one advice of each kind: one entry advice, one exit advice, one getter advice, and so on.

PostSharp reports LA0059 on the second advice of a kind that a group receives:

* Most often, the <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Advices.GroupingAdvice.Group> property gives two advices of the same kind the same group name. The name is shared by all the classes of the aspect, so the two advices can also be declared in a base class and a derived class.
* The obsolete <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Advices.GroupingAdvice.Master> property can do the same, when it names an advice whose group already has an advice of this kind.
* An advice without pointcut and without <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Advices.GroupingAdvice.Group> joins the group of its class that has no advice of its kind yet. When two such advices of the same kind find the same group, for instance two exit advices and one entry advice, both join it.

## Resolution

* Merge the two advices into one method, when they always apply to the same declarations.
* Or give one of them a group of its own: remove its <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Advices.GroupingAdvice.Group> property and give it a pointcut, or give it another <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Advices.GroupingAdvice.Group> value. A group without pointcut applies to the target of the aspect, which must then be a declaration that the advice accepts; otherwise, PostSharp reports <xref:la0049?text=LA0049>. Two groups that both apply to the target of the aspect are reported as <xref:la0257?text=LA0257>.

Two groups on the same declaration are two transformations, so order them with an <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Dependencies.AdviceDependencyAttribute>, which can name any advice of a group.

In the following example, `CheckArguments` has no <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Advices.GroupingAdvice.Group> property any more: its pointcut starts a group of its own, which runs after the `Logging` group. The class then has two groups without exit advice, so `LogExit` selects the `Logging` group with the <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Advices.GroupingAdvice.Group> property.
