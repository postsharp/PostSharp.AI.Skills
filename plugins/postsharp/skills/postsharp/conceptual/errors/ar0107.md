# AR0107: [Protected] Applied to a Declaration C# Already Restricts

| | |
|---|---|
| **Severity** | Error |
| **Reported by** | PostSharp |
| **Message** | `Cannot use [Protected] on <Kind> <Declaration> because the <Kind> is already private or protected, or belongs to a private or protected type. [Protected] applies only to public or internal declarations.` |

## Cause

The <xref:PostSharp.Constraints.ProtectedAttribute> constraint restricts a `public` or `internal` declaration to the type that declares it and to the types derived from it, as the `protected` keyword would, for a declaration that must be public or internal for technical reasons. When the declaration is `private` or `protected`, or belongs to a nested type that is `private` or `protected`, C# already gives this protection, and PostSharp reports AR0107 on the declaration.

PostSharp itself rejects the most direct cases before this check: applying `[Protected]` to a `private` or `protected` member reports <xref:ps0091?text=PS0091>. ## Resolution

* When the nested type does not need to be visible outside of its containing type and its derived types, remove `[Protected]`: the `protected` keyword already gives the protection.
* When the nested type must be public or internal for technical reasons, change its visibility and keep `[Protected]` on the member.
