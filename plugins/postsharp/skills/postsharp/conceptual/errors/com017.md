# COM017: Different Relationship Attributes on a Property and Its Backing Field

| | |
|---|---|
| **Severity** | Warning |
| **Reported by** | PostSharp.Patterns.Aggregation |
| **Message** | `The backing field of the automatic property <Property> is annotated with [<FieldAttribute>] and the property with [<PropertyAttribute>]. The relationship is [<PropertyAttribute>]; the attribute of the backing field does not change it.` |

## Cause

The <xref:PostSharp.Patterns.Model.AggregatableAttribute> aspect reads the relationship of an automatic property from the attributes of the property. PostSharp reports COM017 when the property has one relationship attribute, such as <xref:PostSharp.Patterns.Model.ReferenceAttribute>, and the `field:` target applies another one, such as <xref:PostSharp.Patterns.Model.ChildAttribute>, to its backing field. The attribute of the property wins, so the two attributes contradict each other and one of them is not what you meant.

## Resolution

Decide which relationship the property has, and apply only that attribute, to the property:

* If the attribute of the property is right, remove the attribute of the backing field.
* Otherwise, replace the attribute of the property with the one of the backing field, and remove the `field:` attribute.
