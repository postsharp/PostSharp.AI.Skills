# PS0091: Attribute Applied to an Excluded Declaration

| | |
|---|---|
| **Severity** | Error |
| **Reported by** | PostSharp |
| **Message** | `Cannot apply the custom attribute "<AttributeType>" to <DeclarationKind> "<Declaration>": the attribute cannot be applied to this kind of declaration (<Reason>).` |

## Cause

The <xref:PostSharp.Extensibility.MulticastAttributeUsageAttribute> of an aspect or of another multicast attribute type can restrict the declarations that the attribute applies to by their visibility, their scope or other attributes, with properties such as <xref:PostSharp.Extensibility.MulticastAttributeUsageAttribute.TargetMemberAttributes> and <xref:PostSharp.Extensibility.MulticastAttributeUsageAttribute.TargetTypeAttributes>. When the attribute is multicast, for example from a class to its methods, PostSharp silently skips the declarations that do not match. When you apply the attribute directly to a declaration that does not match, PostSharp reports PS0091 on this declaration.

`<Reason>` describes the attribute of the declaration that the usage excludes, for example `static member`, `instance member`, `private member`, `abstract member` or `static type`. Some aspects of PostSharp have such a usage: for example, an <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.InstanceLevelAspect> cannot be applied to a static class.

## Resolution

* Apply the attribute to a declaration that its usage accepts, or apply it to the containing type and let multicasting skip the declarations that do not match.
* When you wrote the attribute type, and it can support this kind of declaration, extend its <xref:PostSharp.Extensibility.MulticastAttributeUsageAttribute>.
