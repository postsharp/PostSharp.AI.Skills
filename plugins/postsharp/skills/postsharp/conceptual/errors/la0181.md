# LA0181: Method Boundary Advice Applied to a Constructor of Another Assembly

| | |
|---|---|
| **Severity** | Error |
| **Reported by** | PostSharp |
| **Message** | `Advice "<Advice>" is not valid on the instance constructor of type "<Type>": applying OnMethodEntryAdvice, OnMethodSuccessAdvice, OnMethodExitAdvice or OnMethodExceptionAdvice to an instance constructor of another assembly is not supported. Restrict the advice to methods.` |

## Cause

An aspect can enhance the declarations of a referenced assembly when you multicast it with the <xref:PostSharp.Extensibility.MulticastAttribute.AttributeTargetAssemblies> property. PostSharp does not modify the other assembly: it replaces the calls that the current assembly makes to the target method with calls to a generated method that runs the advices. PostSharp does not support this for constructors, and reports LA0181 when an <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Advices.OnMethodEntryAdvice>, an <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Advices.OnMethodSuccessAdvice>, an <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Advices.OnMethodExitAdvice> or an <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Advices.OnMethodExceptionAdvice> targets an instance constructor of another assembly.

This usually happens by default: the <xref:PostSharp.Extensibility.MulticastAttribute.AttributeTargetElements> of a <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.MethodLevelAspect> include the instance and static constructors, so an aspect multicast to a type of another assembly also reaches the constructors that the current assembly calls. An advice without pointcut has the targets of its aspect. `<Advice>` is an advice method of the group, and the message is reported at its location: the advice that has the pointcut of the group, or else the advice that sets a property of the group, or else the first advice of the group. `<Type>` is the type that declares the constructor.

<xref:la0179?text=LA0179> is the same error for an <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.OnMethodBoundaryAspect> or an <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.OnExceptionAspect>.

## Resolution

* Restrict the aspect to methods: set <xref:PostSharp.Extensibility.MulticastAttribute.AttributeTargetElements> to <xref:PostSharp.Extensibility.MulticastTargets.Method>.
* When the advice selects its targets with another pointcut, such as a <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Advices.MethodPointcut>, exclude the constructors from the pointcut.
* When you own the library, apply the aspect in the library itself, where PostSharp can enhance the constructor.
