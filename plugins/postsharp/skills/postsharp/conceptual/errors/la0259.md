# LA0259: Two Pointcuts in One Group

| | |
|---|---|
| **Severity** | Error |
| **Reported by** | PostSharp |
| **Message** | `The advices <Advices> are in one group and each has a pointcut, but a group has one pointcut, which applies to all its advices. Keep the pointcut on one advice of the group, or give the advices different groups.` |

## Cause

A pointcut, such as <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Advices.MulticastPointcut> or <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Advices.MethodPointcut>, selects the declarations to which a group of advices applies. The pointcut is a property of the whole group, so a group has one pointcut, and you can put it on any advice of the group.

An advice with a pointcut starts a group. Two advices with a pointcut are therefore in two groups, unless they select one group with the <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Advices.GroupingAdvice.Group> property. PostSharp reports LA0259 on each advice of the group when two advices of one group have a pointcut. `<Advices>` lists these advices.

## Resolution

Keep the pointcut on one advice of the group. It applies to all the advices of the group. When the advices must apply to different declarations, remove the <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Advices.GroupingAdvice.Group> property, or give the advices different group names: each pointcut then starts a group of its own. When the class has only one group, the <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Advices.GroupingAdvice.Group> property is not needed: PostSharp puts each advice without pointcut in the group that has no advice of its kind.
