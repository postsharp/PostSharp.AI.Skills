# LA0197: Advice Cannot Semantically Advise an Async Iterator

| | |
|---|---|
| **Severity** | Error |
| **Reported by** | PostSharp |
| **Message** | `Cannot apply advice "<Advice>" to method "<Method>": this advice does not support semantic advising of C# 8 async iterator methods. Set the UnsupportedTargetAction property of the master advice to Fallback or Ignore to suppress this error.` |

## Cause

LA0197 is the counterpart of <xref:la0196?text=LA0196> for aspects built from advice methods (see <xref:advices>). An async iterator is a method that is both `async` and an iterator: it returns <xref:System.Collections.Generic.IAsyncEnumerable`1> or <xref:System.Collections.Generic.IAsyncEnumerator`1> and contains both `await` and `yield return`. By default, PostSharp applies advices to such a method semantically (see <xref:semantic-advising>), but semantic advising of async iterators does not support some advices:

* <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Advices.OnMethodYieldAdvice> and <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Advices.OnMethodResumeAdvice>, in a group of method boundary advices;
* <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Advices.OnMethodInvokeAsyncAdvice>, in a group of method interception advices.

When the group of advices contains one of them and the `UnsupportedTargetAction` property of its master advice, for example <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Advices.OnMethodBoundaryAdvice.UnsupportedTargetAction>, is <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.UnsupportedTargetAction.Fail>, which is the default, PostSharp reports LA0197 on the target method. `<Advice>` is the master advice of the group.

## Resolution

* Set the `UnsupportedTargetAction` property of the master advice. PostSharp reads it from the master advice only, and the advice that sets it becomes the master advice of the group:
  * <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.UnsupportedTargetAction.Fallback> applies the advices non-semantically: they run when the method is called and creates the async enumerator, not during the enumeration, and the yield and resume advices are not called.
  * <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.UnsupportedTargetAction.Ignore> skips the advices for this method.
* For method boundary advices, remove the yield and resume advices if you do not need them: PostSharp then applies the other advices semantically to the async iterator.
* Exclude async iterators from the pointcut of the advices.
