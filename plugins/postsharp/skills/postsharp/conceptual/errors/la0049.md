# LA0049: Advice Without Pointcut Cannot Apply to the Target of the Aspect

| | |
|---|---|
| **Severity** | Error |
| **Reported by** | PostSharp |
| **Message** | `The advice "<Advice>" is in a group without pointcut, so it applies to the target of the aspect, "<Target>", but it cannot be applied to this kind of declaration. Add a pointcut to an advice of the group, for instance [MulticastPointcut].` |

## Cause

A pointcut selects the declarations that a group of advices transforms. PostSharp puts an advice without pointcut in a group of its class: the group of an advice that has a pointcut, when there is one, or else a group without pointcut, which applies to the target of the aspect itself. A group without pointcut works only when its advices accept this kind of declaration. When they do not, PostSharp reports LA0049. The typical case is a method advice, such as an <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Advices.OnMethodEntryAdvice>, in a <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.TypeLevelAspect>: the target of the aspect is a type, and the advice can only transform methods.

`<Advice>` is one advice of the group, and the message is reported on it: the advice that sets a property of the group, or else the first advice of the group. `<Target>` is the declaration to which the aspect is applied.

## Resolution

* Add a pointcut that selects the declarations that the advices accept, for instance a <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Advices.MulticastPointcut> that selects the methods of the target type, or a <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Advices.MethodPointcut> that selects them with code. A group has one pointcut, which can be on any of its advices, and the other advices of the group apply to the same declarations.
* Or make the aspect a <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.MethodLevelAspect> and apply it to the methods, when the advices should transform only the target of the aspect.
