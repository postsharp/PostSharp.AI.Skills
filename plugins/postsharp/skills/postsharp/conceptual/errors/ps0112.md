# PS0112: Aspect Conflict or Unsatisfied Aspect Requirement

| | |
|---|---|
| **Severity** | Error |
| **Reported by** | PostSharp |
| **Message** | `Conflicting aspects on "<Declaration>": <Reason>.` |

## Cause

The dependencies of an aspect class, <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Dependencies.AspectTypeDependencyAttribute>, <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Dependencies.AspectRoleDependencyAttribute> and <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Dependencies.AspectEffectDependencyAttribute>, can do more than order aspects. PostSharp checks them once it has ordered the aspects of a declaration, and reports PS0112 in two cases:

* A dependency with the <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Dependencies.AspectDependencyAction.Conflict> action matches another aspect on the same declaration: another aspect type, another aspect that provides a role, or another aspect that has an effect. With a position, the conflict exists only when the other aspect runs before (`After`) or after (`Before`) the current one. `<Reason>` then says that the first transformation `cannot be used together with`, `cannot be preceded by` or `cannot be followed by` the second.
* A dependency with the <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Dependencies.AspectDependencyAction.Require> action matches no other aspect on the declaration, or none at the required position. `<Reason>` then says that the transformation `must be used together with`, `must be preceded by` or `must be followed by` a transformation that matches the condition.

Dependencies between transformations of the same aspect instance are ignored. PS0112 is an error, or a warning when the <xref:PostSharp.Aspects.Dependencies.AspectDependencyAttribute.IsWarning> property of the dependency is `true`.

## Resolution

* For a conflict, remove one of the conflicting aspects from the declaration, or exclude it from this declaration with <xref:PostSharp.Extensibility.MulticastAttribute.AttributeExclude> when the aspect is multicast. When the conflict depends on a position, change the order of the aspects so that the conflicting aspect is on the allowed side.
* For a requirement, add the aspect that the dependency requires, at the required position.
* When the aspects are yours and the combination is valid, remove the dependency, or give it a position that describes the actual problem.
